Safety Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation (CSAE)
✅ Effective date: May 21, 2026  |  Last updated: May 21, 2026

Luna has a zero-tolerance policy for any content or behavior that exploits, abuses, or endangers minors. We are committed to maintaining a platform free from child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Prohibited conduct (zero tolerance)

  • Any real or simulated depiction of child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE material).
  • Grooming, soliciting, or engaging in sexually explicit communication with a minor.
  • Sharing or requesting content that sexualizes minors.
  • Using Luna to direct minors to off-platform harmful content.
  • Any attempt to bypass age-restriction mechanisms.

2. Age requirements

  • Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use Luna’s live chat and streaming features.
  • We use automated age estimation tools and manual reviews for suspicious accounts.

3. Reporting & review

  • In-app: Report button on any user profile, message, or livestream.
  • Email: l2adiant@gmail.com
  • All reports are reviewed within 24 hours by trained moderators.

4. Response actions

Upon confirmation of any CSAE violation:

  • Immediate and permanent account ban.
  • Preservation of evidence and reporting to National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Cooperation with law enforcement globally.

5. Proactive detection

  • Hashing technology (PhotoDNA & similar) to detect known CSAE content.
  • AI-powered text & image moderation for livestreams and DMs.
  • Metadata analysis to flag suspicious behavioral patterns.

6. User education

We display mandatory safety notices to all new users, including how to recognize and report grooming or exploitation.
