2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account data: username, email address, optional profile picture, birth year (for age compliance).
  • Live content: video & audio streams, chat messages, broadcast titles, and interactive reactions.
  • Support correspondence: when you report an issue or contact customer service.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device identifiers: IP address, Android ID, advertising ID (with your consent), model, OS version.
  • Usage & performance: stream start/end times, chat frequency, crashes, network quality.
  • Camera / Microphone: only accessed during a live broadcast and never stored permanently by Luna servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • ✅ Provide live streaming, chat delivery, and stream stability.
  • ✅ Moderate content – automated filters + human review to block hate speech, abuse, or any content violating our Terms.
  • ✅ Improve safety – detect spam, fraudulent accounts, or underage usage.
  • ✅ Send critical updates – policy changes, security alerts (no marketing without permission).
  • ✅ Analyze crashes and optimize app performance.

5. Data Retention

  • Active account: data stored until you delete your account.
  • Chat messages & stream metadata: automatically removed 30 days after a stream ends.
  • Video content: Luna does not record or store live streams unless you manually choose to save a clip (clips stored for 60 days).
  • Deleted accounts: fully erased within 60 days, except anonymized analytics.
  • Crash logs & debugging: retained up to 90 days.

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, etc.)

Depending on where you live, you can:

  • 🔍 Access – get a copy of your data.
  • ✏️ Correct – update inaccurate info.
  • 🗑️ Delete – erase your account and associated data.
  • ⏸️ Restrict – limit processing while a complaint is reviewed.
  • 📦 Portability – receive your data in a structured format.
  • 🚫 Opt out of "sales" – we do not sell data, so no action needed.

To exercise any right, email privacy@luna-stream.app. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Policy & Age Limits

Luna is intended for users aged 13+ (or higher if required locally, e.g., 16 in certain regions). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover an underage account, we will delete it immediately. If you are a parent/guardian and believe your child under 13 uses Luna, contact safety@luna-stream.app.

All live streams and chat rooms are moderated via automated filters + reporting. Suspected child exploitation material is reported to NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children).

8. Security Measures

  • 🔐 Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 for all API, WebSocket, and WebRTC signaling.
  • 💾 Encryption at rest: Supabase database encrypted using AES-256.
  • 🛡️ Access control: only essential engineers have production access, audited regularly.
  • ⚠️ However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Use Luna on trusted networks.

9. International Data Transfers

Luna operates globally. Your data may be processed in the United States (LiveKit), EU/Frankfurt (Supabase region configurable), and other locations where our partners operate. For EEA/UK users, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure adequate protection.
